The problem

ITAD in Japan is harder than most HQ teams expect.

Foreign multinationals often discover these challenges only after the project starts.

Dual compliance gap

Global ITAD vendors know NIST but not Japanese waste management law. Local vendors know Japanese law but not your HQ's reporting requirements. Neither bridges both.

Language barrier

Logistics teams, building managers, recyclers, and local authorities communicate only in Japanese. Your HQ needs English reporting, certificates, and audit documentation.

Vendor fragmentation

Coordinating logistics, data destruction, recycling, and reporting across 3-5 separate vendors creates gaps. Serial numbers get lost. Certificates arrive late. Audit trails break.

Our approach

One accountable partner. Both compliance frameworks. Both languages.

Founded by operators with deep ITAD experience in the Japan market.

Dual compliance by default

Every project satisfies both NIST 800-88 data destruction standards and Japanese waste management law (廃棄物処理法). No gaps between what your HQ expects and what Japan requires.

Bilingual project management

Your HQ gets English-language reporting, certificates, and status updates. Our field teams coordinate with Japanese logistics partners, building management, and recyclers in Japanese.

Single point of accountability

We orchestrate pickup, data destruction, recycling, and certification through our vetted fulfillment network. You deal with one partner, not five. Serialized chain of custody from pickup to certificate.
