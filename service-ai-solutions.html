Managed AI Operations

Your Dedicated AI Agent Team

We deploy a team of specialized AI agents that work 24/7 inside your business. Sales prospecting, customer support, email triage, market research, and workflow automation — handled by dedicated agents with human-in-the-loop governance. You stay in control. They never sleep.

Capabilities

Everything your AI team can do.

AI Agent Teams

Dedicated agents with specific roles — Sales, Support, Operations, Marketing, and Tech. Each agent has a personality, expertise area, and toolkit. They collaborate, share context across conversations, and escalate to humans when needed. Bilingual in English and Japanese.

Command Center Dashboard

Real-time visibility into everything your agents are doing. Leads discovered, emails drafted, tasks completed, web research performed — all in one dashboard. Full audit trail for every action. Monitor agent activity, review pending approvals, and track performance metrics.

Smart Routing & Cost Control

Complex tasks use cloud AI for speed and accuracy. Simple, repetitive tasks stay on local AI for privacy and zero marginal cost. Sensitive data never leaves your environment. You only pay for the intelligence that matters — no wasted tokens, no runaway bills.

Governance & Safety

Council Chamber for multi-agent decisions — agents discuss and vote before taking action. Human approval required for outbound emails and sensitive operations. Complete decision trail for compliance. First-Principles methodology: plan first, act second. No surprises.

How It Works

From discovery to autonomous operations.

1

Discovery

We analyze your existing workflows, map your data flows, and identify the highest-impact automation opportunities. Which tasks eat the most time? Where do leads fall through the cracks? We find out.

2

Architecture

We design your agent team — which roles, which tools, which guardrails. Sales agent for prospecting? Support agent for email triage? Operations agent for reporting? We define the plan, the approval flows, and the escalation rules before writing a single line of code.

3

Deploy

We set up your Command Center, configure each agent with their specific personality and toolkit, and integrate with your email, CRM, and business tools. Your agents start working immediately — with human approval gates on all critical actions.

4

Optimize

Agents learn and improve from every interaction. We monitor performance, expand capabilities, add new agents as your needs grow, and deliver monthly reporting on everything your AI team accomplished. Continuous refinement, not set-and-forget.
