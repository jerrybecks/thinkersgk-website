These scenarios are illustrative examples that show how Thinkers GK scopes, coordinates, documents, and hands over common IT service requests in Japan. They are not presented as named customer testimonials.

ITAD · Data Destruction

Multi-site device refresh for a regional bank branch closure

Financial services · 8 branches across 4 prefectures · 1,200+ devices

課題

Strict regulatory requirements demanded certified data destruction documentation for every device. The client had no internal logistics capability to coordinate simultaneous multi-site pickups, and the 60-day window created pressure across legal, IT, and facilities teams.

対応方針

We coordinated vetted regional partners for on-site work, maintained a single chain-of-custody log across all 8 locations, and produced per-device destruction certificates. One weekly status report was delivered in English and Japanese to the project owner and compliance lead.

結果

All 8 sites completed within the 60-day window. Zero documentation gaps flagged by the compliance audit. The client's legal team confirmed that the destruction certificates met regulatory standards without further remediation.

1,200+ devices certified 8 sites · 60 days Zero compliance gaps EN/JA bilingual reporting
マネージドITサポート

Replacing a fragmented vendor model for a European manufacturer's Japan office

Manufacturing · Tokyo HQ + 2 regional sites · 180 users

課題

The Japan office was running support through three separate vendors with no shared ticketing, inconsistent response times, and reporting that headquarters in Germany could not interpret. The local IT coordinator was spending most of their time translating and chasing vendors instead of doing real IT work.

対応方針

We took over as the single managed support layer, absorbing the existing vendors where useful and replacing the gaps. One ticketing system, one escalation path, and one monthly report in English and Japanese. The local IT coordinator was reoriented to project oversight rather than vendor chasing.

結果

Headquarters received its first interpretable Japan IT report within 45 days of engagement. Average ticket resolution time dropped by roughly 40% in the first three months. The local IT coordinator moved from reactive firefighting to running three scheduled infrastructure projects.

3 vendors → 1 accountable layer ~40% faster resolution HQ reporting restored
ITスタッフィング · SES

Bilingual infrastructure engineers for a US fintech's Japan cloud migration

Financial technology · Tokyo · 6-month initial SES contract · 3 engineers

課題

The client needed engineers who could work in Japanese with the internal IT team and in English with the US-based infrastructure leads. Typical agency channels produced candidates who were technically strong but could not handle bilingual stakeholder communication. The migration had a hard launch date.

対応方針

We structured a requirements brief around the actual bilingual communication scenarios, not just technical skills. We sourced from our network of engineers with confirmed EN/JA delivery experience, conducted structured technical and communication screening, and presented three candidates within 18 days of sign-off.

結果

All 3 engineers started before the required onboarding date. The client renewed for an additional 6 months with two of the three, and one was later offered a direct placement. The migration launched on schedule with no handoff gaps between the US and Japan teams.

3 bilingual engineers placed 18-day sourcing timeline 2 renewals · 1 direct hire On-schedule migration launch
フィールドエンジニアリング · プロジェクト実行

Network infrastructure rollout across 14 retail sites, nationwide

Retail · Sites across Kanto, Kansai, and Kyushu · 8-week delivery window

課題

A retail chain needed all 14 locations upgraded to a new unified network architecture before a busy commercial period. Sites were spread across three regions. The client's internal IT team in Tokyo could not physically cover regional work and had no established field partner network outside the capital.

対応方針

We sequenced the rollout by region to allow the Tokyo team to validate network configuration before each wave moved. Vetted field engineers handled on-site installation at each location, with a standardised checklist and sign-off protocol at each site. A daily status summary was delivered to the IT lead.

結果

All 14 sites completed within the 8-week window, with one site requiring a return visit for a local cable routing issue. The client's IT team reported that the daily summaries were enough to keep regional management informed without any additional internal communication overhead.

14 sites · 8 weeks 3-region coverage Zero missed deadline Daily progress reporting
ディザスタリカバリ · EUSバリデーション

DR exercise validation for a multinational's Tokyo office

Corporate HQ · Tokyo · 4-day exercise window · April 2026

課題

The client ran an annual DR exercise across a weekend failover window and needed independent End User Services (EUS) validation — confirming that all staff could continue working normally after infrastructure failover. Previous exercises had used informal checklists with inconsistent coverage and no formal compliance sign-off.

対応方針

We built a 51-point phased validation checklist covering five sequential phases: pre-DR readiness, live monitoring, post-failover validation, incident logging, and final sign-off. Coverage included LAN/Wi-Fi, VPN, AD login, MFA, Outlook, Teams, Zoom, business-critical applications, file shares, and office infrastructure. All issues found were logged in real time with timestamp and severity classification.

結果

All 51 checklist items were completed across the 4-day window. Zero P1 or P2 issues remained at final sign-off. The client received a compliance-ready package including the completed checklist, incident log, and signed sign-off document — along with a reusable checklist template for future exercises.

51 items · 4 days Zero P1/P2 at sign-off Compliance-ready documentation Reusable checklist delivered
