What we manage

A practical lifecycle view across devices, locations, and handoffs.

This service is built for companies that need more than ad hoc laptop setup. We manage the records and operating actions around laptops, desktops, monitors, thin clients, peripherals, routers, firewalls, and mobile devices where applicable, with enough detail to support refresh planning, retrieval, and ITAD closeout.

Endpoint inventory

Laptops, desktops, monitors, thin clients, and core peripherals aligned to the right user, location, and lifecycle stage.

Network and edge devices

Routers, firewalls, and related site hardware tracked with supplier, handover, and replacement notes where those devices affect office operations.

Ownership and location

Serial number, assigned user, desk or office location, custody status, and local delivery or retrieval checkpoints kept current.

Warranty and supplier context

Supplier, warranty status, purchase timing, and replacement risk notes organized so refresh decisions are not delayed by missing paperwork.

Lifecycle evidence

Delivery records, setup notes, photos, chain-of-custody details, wipe or destruction certificates, and resale or recycling reports where needed.

Lifecycle decision support

OS risk, readiness notes, refresh recommendations, and closeout actions collected in one lane so teams can decide what to replace, keep, retrieve, or retire.

FAQ

Common questions before asset-management work starts.

Do you need us to buy all devices through Thinkers GK?
No. We can work with client-funded procurement, existing suppliers, or a mixed model. The main value is control of records, execution, and closeout, not forcing one sourcing route.
Do you offer hardware rental directly?
This page does not claim a large owned rental pool. Where predictable monthly device cost is important, we can scope a partner-backed Device-as-a-Service route by quotation and manage the surrounding operations.
Can this start as a cleanup project before monthly management?
Yes. Many engagements start with an asset register cleanup or a Windows 11 refresh sprint. Once the baseline is clearer, we can decide whether an ongoing monthly lifecycle lane is justified.
