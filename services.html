Pricing and request flow

A clear route from inquiry to quoted work.

Thinkers GK provides business-to-business IT services on a scoped quotation basis. Final pricing depends on the systems involved, number of users or devices, locations, urgency, security requirements, and whether the work is project-based, recurring support, onsite dispatch, procurement, or asset lifecycle handling.

01 · Inquiry

Initial scope conversation.

We confirm the business issue, systems, users, locations, language needs, target dates, and any security or compliance constraints.

02 · Quote

Written quotation before paid work.

The quotation states scope, deliverables, schedule, assumptions, expenses, tax handling, and payment terms before delivery begins.

03 · Delivery

Implementation with records.

Typical outputs include source code, configuration records, support tickets, scripts, inventory exports, runbooks, certificates, or completion reports.

04 · Billing

Invoice tied to agreed scope.

Billing may be fixed-fee, milestone-based, monthly support, onsite day-rate, procurement-related, or asset-lifecycle based depending on the approved quotation.
