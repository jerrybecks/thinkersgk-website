Offer detail 03

AI Control Tower Pilot

This offer is for teams that want operational AI to be useful, visible, and governable instead of becoming another unmanaged tool.

Discuss this offer
Typical timeline Usually 3 to 6 weeks including workflow triage, pilot setup, supervised run, and review.
Typical deliverables Workflow shortlist, approval matrix, routing baseline, pilot findings, and rollout recommendation.
Budget shape Pilot discovery and setup typically start from JPY 250,000. Broader managed coverage is quoted after the pilot scope is proven.
Not ideal for Organizations looking for fully unmanaged AI autonomy on day one, or teams unwilling to define approval and logging boundaries.

What is included

  • workflow shortlist and risk-band definition
  • approval matrix and escalation logic
  • model and tool routing baseline
  • pilot review and rollout recommendation

Best fit

  • teams already testing AI internally
  • operations that need approvals and auditability
  • environments where human override must stay visible

How it runs

  1. identify candidate workflows
  2. define what AI can and cannot do
  3. pilot under operator oversight
  4. decide whether to expand, limit, or stop
