FAQ

Common questions before a DX program starts.

Do we need a full transformation strategy first?
Not always. In many cases, the right first step is to frame one priority lane clearly enough that the broader program can be sequenced around it.
Is this mainly for leadership or for delivery teams?
Both. The plan has to make sense at management level, but it also has to be usable by the teams who will actually execute it.
What makes DX programs stall?
Weak ownership, vague priorities, and too many parallel changes at once are the most common reasons.
